BMW X3 M40i: The Perfect Daily Driver

Are you tired of bland, uninspired cars that are built to get from A to B and nothing more? Have you always wanted a performance car but don't want to live with the compromises of a sports car? Do you also need a comfortable cruiser for road trips, with all the technology to keep you safe along the way? Sitting at the intersection of elegance and power is the BMW X3 M40i, a car that can go from quiet cruiser to screaming performance car at the push of a button.

I already know what you're thinking: "BMWs are unreliable, I'll end up spending a forture in maintenance." To that I say, it's not 2008 anymore. The M40i uses BMW's B58 engine, which has been in use in various applications for nearly a decade, and has been lauded as a significant improvement over BMWs previous engines. The B58 is even used in Toyota's Supra, if it's good enough for reliability-obsessed Toyota, it's good enough for me. Additionally, Consumer Reports has had BMW in the Top 10 brands for Predicted Reliability for the past few years, with the X3 specifically called out as one of the best midsized Luxury SUVs.

The key to buying any German luxury car is to buy used. These cars depreciate sharply in the first few years after initial purchase, meaning you can get a four to five year old model for the same price as a new RAV4 or CR-V. If you're the kind of person I described above, this is tremendous value for what you're looking for. You can easily find pre-LCI (lifecycle impulse, BMW's term for a facelift) with low miles for less than $40k, this is an SUV with every tech feature you could ask for and the same 0-60 time as a Scat Pack Challenger for the same price as a top-trim CR-V.
